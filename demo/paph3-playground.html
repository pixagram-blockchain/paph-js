Compare-time knobs

P1 — moving these re-decides without re-hashing

This is the whole argument for the larger budget. In v2 every one of these was baked into the wire at hash time, so getting one wrong meant re-hashing the corpus to find out. Here they are pure compare-time choices: the wires above do not change.

Runs entirely in this page — nothing is uploaded. The engine is paph3.js, the same integer reference implementation the node harness tests. Two known gaps, both honest: a colour-inverted copy is carried by the structural channels only (the geometric half cannot fold inversion for free — inverting flips every descriptor bit but also rotates the orientation 180°, so the steered pattern samples the other side of the keypoint), and thresholds are still derived from a 16-work corpus rather than from real moderation reports.
