Load works, measure every pair once through compare_v41, then tune the container-2 profile — nine per-channel tables, thresholds, weights — and watch the whole corpus re-decide instantly. The nine curves are the point of 4.1, kept by 4.2: each structural channel gets its own calibration before the lattice sees it. 4.2 adds a tenth table, geo diversity, which asks how independent the geometric evidence actually was. Certified and at review or better are counted separately everywhere, because they answer different questions. Nothing leaves the browser.
